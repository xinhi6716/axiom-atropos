Axiom Atropos identity assets —2026-10-27

The Axiom Atropos shears/thread symbol and header compositions are original editable SVG constructions for xinhi6716, within the selected Celestial Order identity. They are not traced from external logos or historical religious artwork. Original first-party geometry is distributed under this repository's MIT LICENSE. No historical authenticity, trademark clearance or endorsement is asserted.

assets/icon.png is rendered directly from assets/icon.svg. The responsive header SVGs contain outlined DejaVu Serif/Sans/Sans Mono glyphs; no font binary is bundled. Font notice follows. Upstream pstack-resources artwork, credits and licenses are unchanged and are not relabelled as Axiom artwork.

This is an identity transition from Axiom Forge to the selected name Axiom Atropos, not a repository, plugin identifier, host display-name or command migration.
